Balangi Bandha Park

Boats, lawns and hills

About

PlaceCollege Road, Nayagarh
DistanceAbout 2 km from the town centre
EntryFree
Best forFamilies, evening walks, boating

Balangi Bandha Park is Nayagarh’s favourite green escape — a hill-backed park built around a reservoir (bandha) on College Road, about 2 km from the town centre. The Nayagarh bus stop is around 1 km away and Nayagarh Town railway station about 2 km.

Green hills rise behind the lawns, and the central water body is used for boating when water levels allow. Children can play on colourful slides, swings and see-saws, while adults use the open-air gym, walk the tree-lined paths and admire the flower beds. A pair of swans is a favourite with visitors.

Entry is free and photography is allowed. Late afternoon to sunset is the best time to visit, and it is wise to leave before dark. Reports differ on opening hours, and parts of the park were still being developed, so check locally.

For Families

The park is built for easy afternoons: a large playground, an open gym, picnic-friendly lawns and a lake to look at or sail on. Carry a water bottle, and note that boating depends on the season’s water level.

Nearby

Travel sites list Kantilo Nilamadhab Temple, Ladoo Baba Temple, Raghunath Jew Temple, Kuanria Dam, Baramul Gorge and Maninaga Hill as popular day trips from Nayagarh.
