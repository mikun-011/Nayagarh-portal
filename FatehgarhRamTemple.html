Fatehgarh Ram Temple

A temple on the hill

About

DeityLord Rama
PlaceFatehgarh village, Bhapur block
DistanceAbout 40 km from Nayagarh, 65 km from Bhubaneswar
Height165 feet

High on a hill near Fatehgarh village in Bhapur block, the Ram temple is one of Nayagarh’s newest and most striking landmarks. It stands about 40 km from Nayagarh town and roughly 65 km from Bhubaneswar, and its tall spire rises above the hilltop and the forests below.

The temple is built of Baulamala stone and is 165 feet tall. Villagers formed the Shri Ram Seva Parishad to lead the project, and the money came from donations by villagers and devotees across Odisha — over Rs 10 crore. Work began in 2017 and employed more than 150 workers.

The inauguration was set for 22 January 2024, the day of the Pran Pratishtha at Ayodhya. Before the ceremony, village women carried water from the Mahanadi in a Kalash Yatra.

A Hill with Old Stories

Locals say Lord Govardhan has been worshipped on this hill for decades. They also recall that in 1912 the wood used for Lord Sudarshan during Lord Jagannath’s Nabakalebar was collected from Fatehgarh — a link between this quiet hill and the Jagannath tradition of Puri.

The Climb & the View

A broad stairway leads up the hill to the temple courtyard, where small shrines surround the main sanctum and the view stretches across forest and distant ranges. Carry water, wear comfortable shoes and go early in the day, when it is cooler and quieter.
